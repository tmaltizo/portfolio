Ever wondered how to create a video using AI? I decided to put my AI video generation project to the test by creating a short analysis of the Robinhood Gold Card. Here's the behind-the-scenes look at how I built it using multiple AI tools working together.

## The AI Stack

For this project, I combined several cutting-edge AI technologies:

- **Google Flow/Whisk**: Workflow automation and orchestration
- **ChatGPT Object talk MCP**: Intelligent object manipulation and narration

## The Process

### 1. Content Generation
First, I used ChatGPT to analyze the Robinhood Gold Card and generate a script. I basically just copy and pasted the script from my article
### 2. Visual Creation
Using Whisk, I generated custom visuals for the video:
- Card mockups and designs
- Comparison charts
- Animated transitions
- Brand elements

### 3. Workflow Automation
Google Flow orchestrated the entire process:
- Managed the content pipeline
- Coordinated between AI tools
- Handled timing and sequencing
- Ensured quality control

### 4. Narration and Voice
The ChatGPT Object talk MCP handled:
- Natural language narration
- Voice synthesis
- Timing with visuals
- Emotional tone adjustment

## Technical Challenges

Building this wasn't without challenges:

**API Integration**: Getting different AI services to communicate required custom middleware and careful error handling.

**Timing Synchronization**: Ensuring visuals matched narration timing needed precise coordination between tools.

**Quality Control**: Maintaining consistent visual style and voice tone across AI-generated content.

**Performance**: Optimizing the pipeline to generate content efficiently without sacrificing quality.

## The Results

The final video demonstrates what's possible when you combine multiple AI tools:

- **Fully automated content creation**
- **Custom visual generation**
- **Natural narration**
- **Professional editing**
- **Rapid iteration capabilities**

What would normally take hours of manual work was completed in minutes, with the ability to regenerate and refine instantly.
